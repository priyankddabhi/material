## Visuals

- Clean minimal premium app look, static vertical 9:16 frame, no camera pan/zoom/shake.
- Background: very pale blue, almost white-blue, faint pink-blue gradient at top in later frame. One background throughout.
- Foreground: white heavily rounded-corner cards with soft drop shadows, black bold text, vivid green for completed/progress, small red and blue accents.
- Shapes: rounded rectangles/pills, circles for calendar ticks and outro ring, small blue dot as floating accent, grey 3-D dumbbell in one frame.
- Type: bold rounded sans-serif. Large black headings, much smaller black/muted-grey labels inside cards. No letter-by-letter typing seen in captured frames.

## Animation

- Cards arrive slightly rotated/tilted, settle to near-upright, then slide/fade out — floating, layered feel, not flat cuts.
- Pill and profile photo pop in with small scale-up and soft shadow, slight overshoot, not hard appearance.
- Ring outro and creator text crossfade over each other; final fade to pale blue is soft, not a hard cut.
- Progress filling is the core satisfying motion: green striped progress bar largely filled, green tick circles progressively filling a calendar, green ring drawing itself as a growing clockwise arc (not rotating as a whole).
- No large counting number was legible in captured frames; only calendar dates were clearly readable.
- Motion is slow, eased, continuous — cards ease in and out, not snapping. No frantic hard cuts, flashing, or rapid zooms.

## Timing (estimates)

- Changes roughly every 2–4 seconds in sampling.
- Sampled segment from one pill frame to outro lasted roughly 8–10 seconds; full reel is longer, because an earlier calendar frame was seen on first load.

## Why it feels premium

- One limited palette and one background, so eye is not reoriented by a new scene.
- White cards, generous white space, consistent rounded corners and soft shadows.
- Motion staggered and eased: cards, ticks, progress bar, and ring do not all move at once, and settle gently.
- Small deliberate accents — red dates, green ticks/ring, blue dot, small pill — contrast without clutter.
- Bold simple heading gives each composite a single focal point.

## Takeaway (visuals + animation only)

- COPY: progress as a filling arc/bar, not a static filled shape. For us: money flow bar $20M → $80M → $100M filling left to right, and a ring filling around a key number.
- COPY: cards arriving tilted then settling upright — for us: chip, rack, and data-centre cards arriving tilted, then settling, so scenes assemble instead of appearing pre-built.
- COPY: pop/scale with overshoot for small pills — for us: label pills popping in one by one.
- COPY: one background throughout a section, so the eye is not reoriented; change background only at a section break.
- SKIP: pale-blue app palette and fitness cards — translate to dark stage, white cards/boxes, one green accent.
- SKIP: soft slow pace alone — in a finance explainer, pair slow eased fills with a new element every 1–3 seconds, or a slow fill alone will feel still.
