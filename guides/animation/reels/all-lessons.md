## The core rule from Instagram

**Something must move every 0.5–1.0 seconds. Nothing stays still for more than 2 seconds.**

If a frame looks the same at second 1 and second 3, it is dead.

## 2. Font — size, style, timing

- **Change size, don't just move.** Key word scales 0.8 → 1.2 on entry, then settles. Small words stay small, hero word is 3–4× bigger.
- **Change font/weight per idea.** Bold for the number, regular for the explanation, mono for the data. Never one font size for everything.
- **Change colour for meaning only.** Green = money / Nvidia, white = normal, grey = background. One accent colour, not five.
- **Per-character animation.** In Figma Motion terms, seen on Figma's reels: Direction = In, Each = Character, Duration = 1000 ms, Easing = Ease out, with Position + Scale + Opacity + Blur stacked together.
- **Reading time rule (from our repo, still valid):** hold text about (letters ÷ 15) + 1.5 seconds. Short words can move fast; long sentences must stay longer.

## 3. Timing — the 10-second test (for the next test, not made now)

| Time | What the eye sees | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| 0–1 s | HOOK: one big word pops in, scale + blur → sharp | Stops the scroll in 1 second |
| 1–3 s | MOVE: object slides / assembles, slow camera zoom in | Eye follows motion, not a still |
| 3–5 s | CHANGE: new word, new size, new colour | Brain gets a new signal |
| 5–7 s | COUNT: numbers count up, bars grow, domino falls | Numbers moving = proof moving |
| 7–9 s | TWIST: scene flips, splits, or reverses | Surprise, re-hooks attention |
| 9–10 s | PAYOFF: final question, big, hold 1 second | Viewer remembers one line |

**Easing:** fast in, slow out. Never linear, never instant cut for a hero word.
**Stagger:** letters 2–4 frames apart, words 3–6 frames apart, objects 0.2–0.4 s apart.
**Camera:** slow zoom 1.00 → 1.08 over a scene, or a slow horizontal pan. Never a static camera for 20 seconds.
**Sound:** beat-matched. A move lands on a drum beat. No beat = motion feels random.

## 4. What to fix in our next Nvidia video (checklist)

- [ ] Every scene: at least 1 movement per second — a dot travelling, a bar growing, a word scaling, a rack light blinking.
- [ ] Every 3–5 seconds: a size change — hero word bigger, then smaller, then a new hero.
- [ ] Every scene: an entry (scale/blur/position) + an exit (slide/fade/glitch), not just a fade between stills.
- [ ] Numbers animate: $20M → $80M → $100M must count/grow, not appear as a finished bar.
- [ ] Dominoes must fall one by one, 0.3–0.5 s apart, not all tilted at once.
- [ ] Data centre must assemble: chip → rack → row → floor, not appear whole.
- [ ] Camera moves slowly in every scene. No locked camera.
- [ ] Text: max 6–8 words on screen at once. Long narration goes in voice + captions, not on the image.
- [ ] Test first as 10 seconds only, with the above, before making a full 5-minute video again.
