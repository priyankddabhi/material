# REEL-004 — De9MnRho88a — 2026-10-29

**Lesson:** Two base colours + exactly one surprise accent (or one striking effect)
**Filed in:** [colour](../topics/colour.md)

TEACHING: A restrained two-colour palette + just one contrasting accent
colour or one striking visual effect = unforgettable. Not more colours.
EXAMPLES SHOWN: hot pink + white + electric lemon balloon shapes
("PLAY BOLDER" poster); pearl grey + mint + chrome silver metallic
flowers (minimal editorial poster); obsidian black + glacial ice blue +
frosted glass panels ("Frozen bloom" poster).
CAPTION IDEA: mixing colour theory with visual effects makes design
unforgettable.

RELEVANCE: YES — directly usable colour-and-effect formula for product

white headline — instead of flat white background with no accent.
