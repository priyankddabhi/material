# LIGHTING — mind topic

## WEB-003 — 2026-10-29 — product lighting guide (three-point hero light)
SOURCE: https://www.squareshot.com/post/guide-to-light-for-product-photography-expert-tips
RULE: Hero shots use three-point light: key light 45 degrees front-left,
fill 45 degrees front-right at lower power, backlight/rim from behind to
separate the product from the background. Result = dimensional, not flat.
APPLY IN PROMPT: "three-point studio lighting: soft key left, gentle fill
right, rim light behind outlining the product edges."
CHECK: if the product melts into the background, the rim light is missing
— add it before changing anything else.

## NOTE — spotlight glow (from REEL-001, full rule in composition.md)
A soft glow behind the hero works like a spotlight: it tells the eye
where to look. Use it as the rim/backlight element, not as a second hero.
