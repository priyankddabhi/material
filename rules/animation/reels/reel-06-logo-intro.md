## Visuals

- Minimal flat vector logo intro, no live footage, no camera movement. Frame locked off in portrait, centred, generous empty space.
- Strict palette: solid bright royal blue, white, ONE orange accent. No gradients, textures, shadows, or busy backgrounds in frames seen.
- Single centred composition throughout.

## Animation

- Wordmark builds letter by letter, left to right, staggered pop/slide-in with soft overshoot/bounce, not linear.
- Completed wordmark holds briefly, then scales down and shifts slightly to make room for the next line.
- Tagline fades/slides in and grows slightly as it settles, not appearing at full size at once. A small white tilted pill/dot bounces/moves above it as a secondary accent.
- Hard background change blue → plain white, final lockup centred and smaller: blue circular badge with white initial and small orange dot, plus wordmark in blue with orange accent letter.
- Fade to plain white frame at end.
- No draw-on lettering, no morphing, no rotation, no pan/tilt/zoom — all movement is graphics scaling, sliding, and fading in a fixed frame.

## Font and size

- Wordmark largest: soft rounded bold lowercase sans.
- Tagline sharp contrast: much smaller, all-caps, widely tracked sans.
- Final lockup intermediate in size, smaller than peak wordmark, same rounded face.
- Hierarchy — large wordmark, small tagline, restrained lockup — keeps it legible at phone size.

## Timing (estimates)

- Each distinct state held roughly 1–3 seconds, changes every couple of seconds.
- Unhurried, not rapid-cut. Transitions themselves are the animation.

## Why it feels premium

- Strict two-plus-one palette, single centred composition, lots of negative space.
- Consistent easing, staggered letter entrances, brief hold on completed logo before each change.
- Clean uncluttered white end card.
- Premium comes from restraint — no extra graphics, patterns, or effects competing with logo and tagline.

## Takeaway (visuals + animation only)

- COPY: staggered letter/number build with overshoot, then hold, then scale down for the next line. For us: "$100M" builds, holds, scales down, then the explanation line fades in below.
- COPY: one small bouncing accent dot/pill as a secondary motion, so the frame never feels dead while text holds.
- COPY: hard background change only once, at a clear section break (e.g. problem → question), not every scene.
- COPY: size hierarchy large hero, small tracked explanation, restrained final lockup — same face, different size/tracking, not different fonts everywhere.
- SKIP: blue/white/orange palette and logo badge — translate to our dark stage, white text, one green accent.
- SKIP: unhurried 1–3 second holds with no other motion — in a longer explainer, pair each hold with a secondary motion (orbit dot, glow pulse, bar growing) or the hold will feel still, as in our last video.
