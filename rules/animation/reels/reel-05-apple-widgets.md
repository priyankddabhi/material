## Visuals

- Clean Apple-ad-like look, mostly white/light backgrounds with soft blurred drifting colour washes: lime-green/sky-blue around the fitness widget, hot pink/red around the music widget, white-to-pale-blue gradient under the opening title.
- Widgets are Apple-style rounded rectangles/squares in black with white and coloured type, soft drop shadows, subtle 3D depth.
- No live-action footage, no real camera scene — everything is graphic/UI animation.
- One widget at a time, centred, on a clean mostly empty background, generous white space.

## Animation

- Dominant motion is 3D flip/rotation and tilt, not simple left-right slides. One widget seen tilted about 15–20 degrees, another rotating in perspective so its face foreshortens and text compresses.
- Fitness widget sits largely straight and centred, with a gentle almost imperceptible bob/tilt, while its background does most of the moving.
- Backgrounds: soft gradient blobs drift and change hue slowly behind a held widget, giving continuous motion even when the widget is static.
- Transitions: a widget shatters into small coloured shards on black; a red particle dot bursts before one widget; clean fade/cut to a black brand-logo card, then to a final gradient.
- Opening title pops/scales in centred, then gives way quickly to the first widget; no slow fade-in caught.
- No long linear slide in from off-screen, no morphing one widget into another, no dramatic tiny-to-full scale-up in frames caught.
- No photographic camera pan, dolly, zoom, or handheld movement — depth comes from widgets rotating/tilting in 3D against a flat drifting gradient, mimicking a camera orbiting a floating card.

## Font and size

- Opening title: very large bold rounded sans-serif, black on white, roughly a fifth of frame height, centred.
- Widget titles: much smaller clean white sans-serif; artist names smaller grey/white.
- Fitness numbers: medium-large bold, colour-coded, larger than the tiny white capital labels above their charts.
- No serif, script, or decorative type seen; no on-screen caption/subtitle besides widget UI, the single opening title, and the final logo.

## Timing (estimates)

- Opening title roughly 1–2 seconds; rotating widget beats a couple of seconds each; fitness hold longest, several seconds, with only background changing; shard transition and logo card each about 1–2 seconds; final gradient hold a few seconds.
- Feel is not rapid-fire cutting — slow showcase with quick snappy transitions between long calm holds.

## Why it feels premium

- Restraint: one widget at a time, centred, clean empty background.
- Consistent design language: rounded corners, soft shadows, familiar widget layouts, colour-coded rings and charts, quiet logo outro rather than a loud end card.
- Eased 3D motion: widgets tilt, rotate, and settle, not jump or snap; background wash moves slowly underneath, so eye has time to read the UI.
- Clean transitions: shard-break to black and fade to gradient, not hard noisy cuts.
- Palette changes only by drifting background hue to match the widget's accent.
- Typography hierarchy: one very large title, then small legible UI type, numbers made hero inside the fitness widget by size and colour.

## Takeaway (visuals + animation only)

- COPY: 3D tilt/flip entrance for a card, then settle — for us: GPU chip card, rack card, and data-centre card flipping/tilting in, then settling upright, instead of flat cuts.
- COPY: background colour wash drifting slowly behind a held widget — for us: faint green/grey glow drifting behind a held diagram, so a long hold is never fully still.
- COPY: shard-break transition on black at a section break — for us: one shard-break between problem and payoff, not every scene.
- COPY: numbers as hero inside a card by size and colour — for us: "$100M" large and green inside a black card, labels tiny white capitals above charts.
- SKIP: Apple widget layouts, music/fitness content, pink/lime/cyan multi-accent palette — translate to dark stage, white/grey, one green accent.
- SKIP: slow showcase pace alone — pair long calm holds with a drifting background or a filling ring/bar, or a long hold will feel still.
