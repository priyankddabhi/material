## Visual — what it looks like

- Almost all white background, black text, ONE vivid neon-green accent, plus pale-green blurred glows and arcs.
- Very spacious: usually one headline, one small graphic, or one row of labels at a time. Lots of empty space.
- Simple rounded shapes: testimonial cards, rounded pill labels, thin-line icons, green hexagons, thin connector lines, corner brackets, sweeping curved lines.
- No live camera footage, no presenter face. Only tiny avatars inside testimonial cards.

## Motion — how it moves

- Pills do NOT appear at once. They pop/slide in one by one, staggered, then hold as a completed set. This is the slowest, most deliberate passage.
- Headline changes are clean cuts softened by the next element sliding, fading, or scaling in.
- One text line reveals left-to-right.
- Green arcs, glows, and curves drift/sweep across corners and edges, never jump.
- Testimonial cards and icons float/scale gently into place, not hard snaps.
- Second hexagon slides in with visible motion blur — fast slide, blurred, then sharp.
- No camera pan/tilt/zoom. Canvas stays fixed; all movement is graphic scale/slide inside the frame.

## Font and size

- Clean geometric sans-serif throughout. Headlines bold, pill/supporting text regular.
- Hierarchy, not one size: largest = single attention word, medium-large = trying-to-understand headline, medium = audience word, small uniform = pill text, tiny = supporting lines.
- Emphasis in the pill section comes from colour (solid green vs white/black outline), not from changing font size.

## Timing / pacing (estimates)

- New element every 1–3 seconds inside a scene.
- Scene holds roughly 3–5 seconds before changing.
- Pill build is slower because each label is added separately.
- Single-word beat is fastest contrast — one word, huge, then cut.

## Why it feels premium

- Strict palette: white, black, one green, soft pale-green glows only.
- One focal point per frame, generous white space, rounded shapes, thin precise lines.
- Staggered entrances with a settle-and-hold before the next change, not constant flashing.
- Same graphic language repeats (pills, hexagons, line icons, arcs), so the recap feels intentional, not repeated.
- Motion blur on the fastest slide, soft glows, clean end-logo card — no hard cheap cuts.

## Takeaway (copy this, skip that)

- COPY: one focal point per frame, white space, staggered pill/label builds for numbers and benefits, single huge word for a key idea ("Attention" → for us, "LOOP" or "RISK"), motion blur on fast slides, settle-and-hold.
- COPY: colour as emphasis, not size, inside a list — e.g. green for Nvidia money, white/grey for others.
- SKIP: all-white background is not our dark Mix 3 theme — translate the same discipline to dark: black stage, white text, one green accent, pale-green glows only.
- SKIP: do not copy the testimonial cards or the audience icons — they are for a clarity pitch, not a finance explainer. Use the pill-build mechanic for $20M / $80M / $100M instead.
- FIX vs our last video: our scenes held 20–40 seconds with one still image. This reel never holds a scene without a new element for more than 3–5 seconds, and a new element arrives every 1–3 seconds.
