# REEL-001 — DeUYA-mxQub — 2026-10-29

**Lesson:** One place for the eye to start: a glow like a spotlight, one biggest thing
**Filed in:** [composition](../topics/composition.md), [lighting](../topics/lighting.md)

TEACHING: Give the viewer's eye one clear place to start.
- Boring poster → add a soft glow (works "like a spotlight").
- Off poster → make one thing the biggest ("nothing stands out" when
  everything is big; biggest = "where your eye starts").
- Muddy poster → pick a brighter colour (dark-on-dark blends; bright pops).
