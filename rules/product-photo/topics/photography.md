# PHOTOGRAPHY — mind topic

## WEB-002 — 2026-10-29 — photo techniques (depth, lines, thirds)
SOURCE: http://fastercapital.com/content/Visual-storytelling-in-advertising--Photographic-Techniques--Photographic-Techniques-that-Transform-Ad-Visuals-into-Stories.html
RULE: Narrow depth of field isolates the hero (sharp product, soft
background). Leading lines point at the hero or brand. Hero on a rule-of-
thirds intersection for balance.
APPLY IN PROMPT: "shallow depth of field, hero tack-sharp, background softly
blurred; a natural line (table edge, stem, shadow) leading to the hero."
CHECK: if background is as sharp as the hero, the eye has no anchor — redo.
