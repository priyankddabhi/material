# TYPOGRAPHY & FRAME TEXT — mind topic

## GIT-004 — 2026-10-29 — GitHub gist design guidelines (frame type rules)
SOURCE: https://gist.github.com/CarlMungazi/9a8644dd3d8a34e96023b664dec4bcb0
RULE: Title text 1.5x body size; title and body must be DIFFERENT
treatments; never mix emphasis effects (no underline + italic + bold
together); small text needs 4.5:1 contrast against its background.
APPLY IN FRAME: brand caps, headline, CTA = three distinct sizes, one
font family, no mixed effects, dark text on cream only.
CHECK: if headline and CTA look the same size or weight, resize — the
eye must rank them instantly.

## REEL-007 — 2026-10-29 — reel DebxmyyT2OH (study real type in use)
RULE: Taste is hours in front of great work. Judge a typeface by how it
works in a REAL layout, brand, or publication — never from a specimen
sheet alone.
ARCHIVES (free, from the reel): Fonts In Use (type in real layouts);
SVA Milton Glaser Design Study Center and Archives (poster work);
Cooper Hewitt collection (30+ centuries of design objects); Design
Reviewed (historic graphic design + typography archive).
NOTE: the reel's caption says "5" archives but only 4 are named anywhere
in speech, cards, or caption — no 5th is invented here.
APPLY BEFORE DESIGNING: open Fonts In Use, find a typeface already
working in a real poster or label, copy that PROVEN treatment: one large
bold product name, one small clean line (scent / material / price),
limited high-contrast palette (e.g. black + cream + one accent).
NEVER: pick a decorative font from a specimen and crowd the frame with
several type styles.
CHECK: can you point to a real layout your type treatment copies? If
not, you are guessing — go look first.
